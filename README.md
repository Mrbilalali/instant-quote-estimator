# Instant Quote Estimator — Interactive Demo

An interactive demo by **Bilal Ali, AI Automation Expert**: answer 4 questions about a home-services cleaning job and watch the AI build an instant quote in seconds.

- **Live demo:** https://instant-quote-estimator-mr-bilal-alis-projects.vercel.app
- **Book a discovery call:** https://cal.com/mrbilalali/discovery-call
- **LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/mrbilalali

## What it does

Visitor picks home size, bedrooms, service type and frequency for the fictional "FreshNest Cleaning Co.", watches a short "AI is building your quote…" sequence, then gets an illustrative price range with a transparent breakdown (base + bedroom add-on, service multiplier, frequency discount), what is included, what would move the price, and a Cal.com discovery-call CTA.

## Honest framing

This is a **scripted simulation** for demonstration only. FreshNest Cleaning Co. is fictional, all prices are illustrative demo pricing, and the demo places no real quotes, calls, SMS or bookings.
